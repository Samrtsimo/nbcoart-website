Company Profile

Art building materials, made with care

Ningbo Coart Building Material Co., Ltd, a company registered in 2021, focuses on art building materials — designing and supplying sculptures of various types and other art products.

Our service includes:

  • 1
    3D design with artistsWe cooperate with artists to complete the 3D design.
  • 2
    Factory matching & productionWe link with reliable small and mid-sized factories in China, communicate technical details, negotiate and calculate cost, arrange production, control quality, inspect goods and arrange delivery by sea or air.
  • 3
    Our clientsArtists, garden designers, logo designers, art galleries, hotels, building consulting & construction companies, government-related bidders, and personal art lovers.

A good design is a good start — and continued, repeated communication is what makes a perfect art product.

Always welcome for you!
