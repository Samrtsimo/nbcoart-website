Company Profile

From first sketch to final delivery

Ningbo Coart Building Material Co., Ltd (registered in 2021) helps artists, designers and buyers turn a sculpture idea into a finished, delivered product. Our core strengths:

  • 1
    Early-stage sculpture designWe help you develop the design from the very beginning — a sketch, references or just an idea — and complete it into a manufacturable 3D model with the artists.
  • 2
    A network of reliable, professional factoriesWe connect you with many dependable, specialised metal, stone, fiberglass and glass-art factories that match your material, quality and budget.
  • 3
    Order follow-up, inspection & deliveryWe track production with the factory, inspect and accept the goods, and arrange delivery by sea or air — making up for the factory's inexperience.

We work with artists, garden designers, logo designers, art galleries, hotels, building consulting & construction companies, government-related bidders, and personal art lovers.

A good design is a good start — and continued, repeated communication is what makes a perfect art product.

Always welcome for you!
